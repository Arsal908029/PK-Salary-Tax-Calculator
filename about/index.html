About PK Tax Tools

Publisher and editorial team: PK Tax Tools · Updated 15 October 2026.

PK Tax Tools builds free calculators that help people in Pakistan understand how much tax they pay and why. Each tool shows the slab-by-slab working so you can check the answer yourself.

Our experience is in building and maintaining these browser-based tools. We do not claim tax-professional credentials, and the calculators are not prepared or approved by the FBR.

Where the rates come from

Each tool identifies the tax year it covers and links to the references used for its published rates. For official rules, consult the relevant Finance Act and the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR). Third-party summaries are useful cross-checks, not a substitute for the law or official guidance.

Editorial policy

PK Tax Tools is maintained by its site publisher. We do not claim that the tools are prepared or reviewed by a tax professional, and the site is not an official FBR service. When updating a rate table, we record its tax year and update date, check the slab calculations with examples and automated tests, and link to the references used. The calculator explains its assumptions and exclusions so readers can judge whether an estimate fits their situation.

Corrections and updates

We review the rates after changes to the Finance Act and welcome reports about possible errors or unclear explanations. Send a report through the contact page; include the page and the relevant figure, but do not send private financial information. Material calculation changes are checked against the code and tests before publication.

Limits and disclaimer

The tools provide general educational estimates only. They do not account for every income source, credit, exemption or personal circumstance and do not replace advice from a qualified tax professional. Confirm important amounts with official guidance or an adviser before filing or making a financial decision.

Publisher location

PK Tax Tools is an online-only resource and does not have a public walk-in or mailing address. For corrections or questions about the site, use the online contact channel.
