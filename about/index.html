About PK Tax Tools

PK Tax Tools builds free calculators that help people in Pakistan understand how much tax they pay and why. Each tool shows the slab-by-slab working so you can check the answer yourself.

Where the rates come from

Rates are taken from the Finance Act and published summaries, compared across more than one source, and listed on each page with the date they were last updated. We update them after every federal budget.

Limits

The tools are estimates for general information. They do not replace advice from a qualified tax professional or the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR). If you find a mistake, please tell us through the contact page.
