How does the Pakistan salary tax calculator work?

Pakistan's salary income tax generally uses progressive tax bands: different portions of taxable income are taxed at different rates. Moving into a higher band does not mean that all of your income is taxed at that higher rate. The calculator annualizes salary, applies the selected year's salaried rates to taxable income, then shows annual tax and an average monthly amount.

Monthly salary, annual tax, and take-home pay

When monthly salary stays the same throughout the year, annual gross salary is generally the monthly amount multiplied by 12. For example, a monthly salary of PKR 150,000 is PKR 1,800,000 a year before bonuses or adjustments. This calculator estimates an average monthly tax deduction by dividing estimated annual tax by 12; actual payroll withholding may differ.

Take-home salary is the amount remaining after deductions. A simplified view is gross salary − estimated income tax. Other deductions, such as employee contributions or benefits, are not included in this calculator, so your actual net pay may be different.
