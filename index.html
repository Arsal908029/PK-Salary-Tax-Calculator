A clearer estimate of your take-home pay

A new job offer or a change in pay can make it hard to know what will reach your bank account. This free calculator turns an annual set of rates into an estimate you can compare with your payslip. Enter your gross pay, choose monthly or yearly, and review the result before making a budget. You can also work backwards from a take-home amount to estimate the gross pay you may need.

How to use the calculator

Start with the salary amount before deductions. Choose the period that matches your figure, then check the result for an annual total, an average monthly deduction, and estimated pay after withholding. If your package includes a bonus or an exempt allowance, open the extra fields and enter those amounts separately. Leave them blank when you are unsure; guessing can make the estimate less useful. The offer comparison can help you review two packages on the same basis.

The calculation applies the rate for each band only to the part of your taxable income inside that band. Moving into a higher band does not mean that all of your pay is charged at its rate. The page shows the working so you can see how the estimate was reached, rather than relying on a single unexplained figure.

What the estimate includes

This tool is for employment income and the published salaried rates for 2026-27. The period runs from 1 July 2026 through 30 June 2027. Your actual deduction may differ if you have other income, credits, benefits, exemptions, or a change in the rules. The calculator does not file a return, confirm your eligibility for an allowance, or replace payroll calculations. Check the relevant Finance Act and Federal Board of Revenue guidance for your situation.

Rates, privacy, and updates

Rates can change when a Finance Act is passed. We review the figures after the federal budget and identify the year shown by the calculator. Before relying on an estimate, compare it with current official guidance or ask a qualified adviser. Read our editorial policy and rate-checking method, learn more about the publisher, and review the privacy policy.

Your salary figures are processed in your browser to produce the estimate; the calculator does not send them to a server. Avoid sharing personal details when asking a question. If you spot a calculation issue or want to suggest an improvement, use the contact page.
